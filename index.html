About

Moses has been acting for nine years, beginning his stage journey in 2017 as a servant in The Shrew, a modern Sydney adaptation of Shakespeare’s The Taming of the Shrew. Since then, he has continued to develop his craft through formal training, completing a Beginner’s Course in Screen Acting at ScreenWise before graduating with a Diploma of Creative Arts in Acting from JMC Academy. Passionate about both stage and screen, Moses has continued building his experience with professional projects, including a background role in a Superwog production that caught the attention of the duo’s loyal fanbase.
